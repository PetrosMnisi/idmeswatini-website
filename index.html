      History

      From BOLESWA to IDM Eswatini

      The Institute of Development Management was founded in 1974 as a joint initiative of Botswana, Lesotho and Eswatini (then Swaziland), with a campus in each country. For five decades it has trained managers, administrators and health professionals across the region.

      In 2025 IDM Eswatini began operating as an independent institution, and it is now building its own academic, staff development and internationalisation strategies.

      1974

      IDM established to serve Botswana, Lesotho and Swaziland.

      2024

      IDM Eswatini exhibits at the Eswatini International Trade Fair; partnership with the Institute of People Management (IPM).

      2025

      IDM Eswatini becomes an independent institution.

      2026

      Joins Business Eswatini; launches staff development, employability and internationalisation initiatives.

      Step by step

      How to apply

      01 · Apply within the application window

      Complete the online form, upload your documents and proof of payment of the application fee. Say whether you want to apply for exemption. You get a reference number such as IDM27-7KQ4M immediately.

      02 · Document check

      Admissions verifies your documents and tells you in the applicant portal if anything is missing or incorrect.

      03 · Payment check

      Finance verifies your proof of payment. Applications with an invalid proof of payment are dropped.

      04 · Decision

      The Head of Department admits or declines your application. You see the decision in the applicant portal straight away.

      05 · Student number & letter

      Admissions issues your student number, and you download your acceptance letter from the applicant portal.

      Start application

      Connecting scholarship, teaching and practice

      Established in April 2026, the Directorate of Research, Innovation and Postgraduate Studies leads the commitment of the Institute of Development Management (IDM) Eswatini to research, innovation and postgraduate studies.

      Your voice

      Complaints and appeals

      1. Talk to the people involved. Most concerns are resolved quickly by your lecturer, Head of Department or the office concerned.
      2. Put it in writing. Send your complaint through the enquiry form (choose the topic that fits) or to Student Affairs. You receive a reference and a written reply.
      3. Academic appeals. Appeals about marks, results or progression follow the assessment and appeals procedures; ask the Registry for the form and the closing date.
      4. Escalation. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, ask for the matter to be reviewed by the Registrar or the Office of the Executive Director.
      5. External recourse. Learners who have used the internal procedures may contact the Eswatini Higher Education Council about matters within its mandate.
      Data protection

      Privacy notice

      What we collect

      The information you enter in our forms: applications and documents, enquiries, bookings, alumni registration, training requests and job applications.

      Why we use it

      Only to process what you asked for: assessing applications, replying to enquiries, managing bookings and communicating decisions to you.

      How it is protected

      Personal information is stored encrypted in the IDM database. Uploaded documents are private and are seen only by the staff who process them.

      Cookies

      The website uses only the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie when you log in and a cookie that remembers your browser for your own forms. No advertising cookies are used.

      Your rights

      You may ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Contact the Registry at admissions@idme.ac.sz.

      Who sees it

      Admissions, Finance, Heads of Department and the Registry, each only for their part of the process, under role-based access control.

      Accessibility

      Accessibility statement

      We want everyone to be able to use this website. You can make text larger or smaller with the A− and A+ buttons, switch between light and dark colours, move around with the keyboard, and every photo has a description for screen readers. If something does not work for you, tell us through the enquiry form and we will help and fix it.
